# Lead Research — Deep Profile Enrichment

## Goal
Build a complete research dossier on a LinkedIn lead before outreach begins.

## Steps

1. **Profile Overview**:
   - Current role, company, tenure
   - Career trajectory (last 3 roles)
   - Education and notable credentials

2. **Recent Activity** (last 30 days):
   - Topics they post about
   - Posts with highest engagement
   - Comments they've left on others' content
   - Tone: thought leader / consumer / sharer?

3. **Company Intel**:
   - Company size, industry, funding stage
   - Recent company news (hiring, product launches, press)
   - Likely pain points based on company stage

4. **Personalization Hooks** (pick top 3):
   - A specific post or comment to reference
   - A shared connection or group
   - A recent company milestone or challenge
   - A skill or interest that aligns with your offer

5. **Outreach Recommendation**:
   - Suggested angle for connection request
   - Best value to offer on Day 3
   - Likely objections to anticipate
