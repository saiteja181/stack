## LinkedIn Outreach Rules

### Personalization
- Always research the lead's recent posts, job change, or company news before writing a message
- Reference something specific about the person — never send generic outreach
- Keep connection request notes under 300 characters
- Follow-up messages must add value, not just check in

### Messaging Cadence
- Day 1: Connection request with personalized note
- Day 3 (after connect): Value-first message — share insight, article, or resource
- Day 7: Soft ask — offer a call, demo, or resource
- Day 14: Final follow-up — keep the door open, never pressure

### Tone & Voice
- Professional but conversational — no corporate jargon
- First-person and direct — avoid passive voice
- Lead with their pain point, not your product/service
- Always end with a clear single call-to-action

---

## Content Creation Rules

### AI Trend Reports
- Source from: LinkedIn trending topics, Google Trends, industry newsletters
- Format: Hook → 3 key insights → takeaway + CTA
- Keep posts under 1300 characters for mobile readability
- Use line breaks every 2-3 sentences for visual breathing room

### Inbox Organisation
- Priority 1: Replies to outreach (respond within 2 hours)
- Priority 2: Connection requests with notes (respond same day)
- Priority 3: General messages (respond within 24 hours)
- Archive: Promotional messages, newsletters
